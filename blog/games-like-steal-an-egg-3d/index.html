The 8 best games like Steal an Egg 3D

1. Smash and Steal an Egg!

Instead of only stealing, you smash eggs with a pickaxe, grab the animal that hatches and outrun the boss guards to carry it home. There are 9 zones, from the Forest to the Galaxy, and 72 animals ranging from common to legendary. You can train your speed on the treadmill, buy trails and upgrade your pickaxe to crack tougher eggs.

Controls: WASD to move, left mouse to swing, right mouse to look around, Space to jump and E to interact.

2. Break the Egg and Run away!

A Roblox-style egg heist where giant blocky animals guard the eggs. Break an egg, grab the pet that hatches and run it home before the plot's guard catches you. Pets in your pen earn coins, which you spend on pickaxes, treadmill training and a bigger pen. There are ten lands, starting from the Forest, and 84 unique blocky pets to fill your Index.

Controls: WASD to move, Space to jump, right mouse drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, click to swing the pickaxe, hold E to pick up a pet and Q to drop it.

3. Break and Steal Eggs

This one adds rivals to the mix. You smash eggs, steal the pet and run back past the guard and other players. You can stun rivals with a bat and steal their pets, and you can merge three pets into a stronger one. Placing pets in your pen earns money every second.

4. Steal An Egg - Grow Animals

A chase-focused game where you sneak into guarded nests, grab eggs and bring them back to your safe zone before the angry guards catch you. You place eggs in the pen, wait for them to hatch and collect pets in five rarity tiers: common, rare, epic, legendary and mythical. It supports eight languages, including German, Spanish, French, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese and Russian.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, right mouse button to look around and Space to jump. On mobile, use the joystick and the jump button in the bottom right corner.

5. Obby: Steal an Egg - The Great Escape

This is a competitive race for the coolest pet collection. You steal eggs from NPC creatures and other players, hatch pets that earn coins passively and spend the money on your treadmill and base. Speed is the key: fast thieves steal unnoticed and escape slow bosses easily. Look out for rare eggs with mutations and unusual sizes.

How it works: steal eggs with the steal button, hatch them in the incubator at your base, train on the treadmill and upgrade your base.

6. Egg Thief

Egg Thief is a 3D stealth chase you play with one finger. Sneak up on a sleeping guardian, grab its egg and carry it home before it wakes up. A short drag tiptoes quietly, while a long drag sprints and makes noise. Common eggs lie at the edge of the nest, but the rare ones sit right under the guardian's nose and are heavy. It supports 19 languages.

Controls: on desktop, WASD or arrow keys to move and Shift to sneak. Eggs are picked up and delivered automatically, which makes this the easiest game on the list.

7. Steal All Eggs

Explore colorful 3D areas and collect pets at your own pace. You can choose between two single-player modes. In Steal An Egg, you take an egg from a nest, avoid guards and bots and carry it back to your pen. In Break An Egg, you use a pickaxe to break eggs and pick up the pets. Special eggs appear during the night event.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, E or Enter for the nearby action, and the mouse wheel to zoom.

8. Steals Eggs From Animals

Run into an animal's zone, steal its egg and escape back to the safe zone. The farther you run, the cooler the animals get. The game has seven zones and more than 25 unique animals, and you earn money from the animals in your pen.

Controls: WASD to move, hold the right mouse button to rotate the camera, Space to jump and hold E to interact.

Beginner tips for any egg-heist game

  • Train your speed first. In several of these games, guards catch anyone slower than the zone's recommended speed. Use the treadmill before moving to a farther zone.
  • Get pets into your pen quickly. Pets in the pen earn coins, and those coins pay for faster progress.
  • Upgrade one thing at a time. Better pickaxes, a bigger pen and treadmill upgrades all help, but a speed upgrade usually unlocks the next area fastest.
  • Learn the drop and grab keys. E often picks up or interacts, and Q or R drops a pet, so you can escape when a guard gets close.
  • Be careful with in-game purchases. Several of these games offer optional purchases or reward videos. You can play without paying.

Which game should you play first?

If you want the easiest start, try Egg Thief. If you want a long progression with many zones and animals, pick Smash and Steal an Egg! or Break the Egg and Run away!. If you like a bit of rivalry, go for Break and Steal Eggs. Players who enjoy speed and treadmill training will like Obby: Steal an Egg - The Great Escape.
